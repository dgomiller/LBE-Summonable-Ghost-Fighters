[size=5][b]LBE: Summonable Ghost Fighters[/b][/size]
[color=#D4D4D8]A spin-off, standalone companion to [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/windrose/mods/535][b]Living Base Enhanced[/b][/url] — summon a translucent ghost crewman or a corrupted Senkamati ally on demand, have it follow and fight alongside you, and watch it dissipate into mist a couple of minutes later. Nothing it spawns is ever saved, so nothing lingers — every summon is a short-lived, disposable escort, not a permanent addition to your base. Works completely on its own; Living Base Enhanced is not required.[/color]

[size=4][b]What it does[/b][/size]
[color=#D4D4D8]
[b]Summon a ghost sailor (Home)[/b] — one random-look crew member (Buccaneers Musketeer, Sailor, Sergeant, a Brethren of the Coast woman, or the default Player Crew look) appears as a translucent ghost and starts following you.

[b]Summon a corrupted Senkamati ally (End)[/b] — one Senkamati (Warrior, Hunter, Caster, or Thrall, picked at random) appears with the same ghostly look, genuinely friendly to you, and keeps its real native combat AI — it will actually fight hostiles alongside you, not just stand there. A Caster can pick targets and cast, and its totems belong to you.

[b]An optional Grenadier[/b] — turn on [i]Grenadier Summons[/i] and each Home press has a chance (10% by default, adjustable) to summon a Blackbeard Grenadier instead of a sailor. [b]Off by default[/b], because its grenades do a lot of damage, including to your own base.

[b]Never in your way[/b] — a summon that falls too far behind teleports back beside you, always [i]behind[/i] you, never in front. How far is a slider (10 to 500 m, default 30 m).

[b]They dissipate, they don't just vanish[/b] — after their lifetime runs out (120 seconds by default), every summon plays a short dissipating-mist effect at its own feet before disappearing, and an on-screen message tells you how many are left. If one falls in a fight, you get a message for that too.

[b]A trailing ground light[/b] follows every summoned ghost, so you can track them at a glance even in a crowd or at range. Prefer their normal look? Turn on [i]Disable Ghost Effect[/i] and summons keep their own appearance.

[b]Capped, with a warning[/b] — up to 5 summons (sailors, Senkamati and Grenadiers combined, configurable) can be active at once; summoning past the cap shows an on-screen "Max Ghosts Summoned" message instead of silently doing nothing.

[b]Combat-aware following[/b] — a summon yields to its own combat AI the moment it's actually fighting something.

[b]Completely non-persistent by design[/b] — nothing this mod spawns is ever written to a save file. Reload the game and every ghost is gone, whether or not its timer had run out yet.
[/color]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[color=#D4D4D8]
[list=1]
[*]Install UE4SS (latest experimental / RE-UE4SS build) into Windrose, same as any other UE4SS mod for this game.
[*]Extract the download so the mod folder sits at:
...\Windrose\R5\Binaries\Win64\ue4ss\Mods\SummonGhostSailors\
(you should have ...\Mods\SummonGhostSailors\Scripts\... inside it).
[*]Enable it: open ...\ue4ss\Mods\mods.txt and add the line:
SummonGhostSailors : 1
[*]Launch the game, load into a world, and press Home or End to try it.
[/list]
Optional: edit Scripts\config.lua (plain Lua, well-commented) to change lifetimes, the max-active cap, the roster, or either key.
[/color]

[size=4][b]Controls[/b][/size]
[color=#D4D4D8]
Home — Summon one random-look ghost sailor (or a Grenadier, if enabled)
End — Summon one corrupted Senkamati ally
(Home and End sit right next to each other, by design.) Remap either in Scripts\config.lua (Config.SUMMON_KEY / Config.SENKAMATI_KEY), or via Windrose Mod Settings if installed (see below).
[/color]

[size=4][b]Optional: Windrose Mod Settings Support[/b][/size]
[color=#D4D4D8]If you also have [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/windrose/mods/442][b]Windrose Mod Settings[/b][/url] by [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/windrose/users/77413713][b]IceBoxStudio[/b][/url] installed, this mod registers itself with it automatically. From the game's native Settings > Mods screen you can change:
[list]
[*]Sliders: Ghost Lifetime (10–500 seconds), Teleport Distance (10–500 m), Max Simultaneous Ghosts (1–15), Grenadier Chance (1–50%).
[*]Toggles: Ground Light Effect, Disable Ghost Effect, Grenadier Summons.
[*]Keybinds: both summon keys.
[/list]
[b]Changes take effect after restarting the game.[/b][/color]

[color=#D4D4D8]Entirely optional both directions: this mod works exactly the same without Windrose Mod Settings installed, using the values in Scripts\config.lua.[/color]

[size=4][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[color=#D4D4D8]
[list]
[*]Windrose (Kraken Express) — single-player. Built against the current Early Access build.
[*]UE4SS — latest experimental / RE-UE4SS build.
[*]No other mods required — this is fully standalone, including from Living Base Enhanced.
[*]Note: this is an unofficial mod, so a game patch may change internal class paths (all centralized in config.lua for quick fixing).
[/list]
[/color]

[size=4][b]Known Limitations[/b][/size]
[color=#D4D4D8]
[list]
[*]Out of combat, summons walk at a fixed pace and cannot run to catch up — they teleport behind you instead (see Teleport Distance).
[*]Because it's an unofficial mod, a game patch can change internal class/material paths and temporarily break the ghost look or a summon. Everything version-dependent lives in config.lua.
[*]The Senkamati ally shares its faction copy with the friendly-faction technique this mod's sibling project (Living Base Enhanced) also uses — if a future game patch changes how NPC factions work, both mods would need the same fix.
[/list]
[/color]
