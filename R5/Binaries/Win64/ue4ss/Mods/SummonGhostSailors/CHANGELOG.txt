LBE: Summonable Ghost Fighters — Changelog
(internal mod folder/id: SummonGhostSailors)

====================================================================
1.1.0 (2026-10-02)
====================================================================
ADDED
- Thrall joins the Senkamati roster (End now summons a Warrior, Hunter, Caster or Thrall).
- Optional Grenadier: when "Grenadier Summons" is on, a Home press has a chance (10% by default,
  a slider in Settings > Mods) to summon a Blackbeard Grenadier instead of a sailor. OFF by
  default because its grenades do heavy damage.
- New setting "Disable Ghost Effect" (off by default): summons keep their normal look instead of
  the ghost reskin.
- New "Teleport Distance (m)" slider (10 to 500 m, default 30 m): a summon that falls this far
  behind teleports back beside you. It now always lands behind you, never in front.
- On-screen messages when a summon dissipates or falls in a fight, with how many are left
  (Config.DESPAWN_NOTIFY).

CHANGED
- The ghost look now matches Living Base Enhanced's "Make Ghost" preset (ghost skin, hair and
  armor materials; blank eyes and mouth).
- Senkamati allies and Grenadiers get their owner set to you, so a Caster can pick targets and
  cast and its totems belong to you.
- Out of combat, summons walk at a fixed pace whatever speed is set, so the follow-speed tuning
  is switched off and the teleport does the catching up.
- A new summon is left completely alone for its first 3 seconds while it finishes building.
- Every summon (sailor, Senkamati, Grenadier) expires after the ghost lifetime and counts toward
  the shared cap.
- Settings > Mods now lists sliders, then toggles, then keybinds, with fuller descriptions.
  Every change there needs a game restart. Registered with the mod's Nexus page (548).

FIXED
- Stability: the follow, teleport, despawn and message code now run on the game thread (a crash
  was seen at the first despawn when this work ran on a timer thread).
- The Grenadier now loads reliably (its class is loaded through the asset registry and the
  summon waits for it).

====================================================================
1.0.1 (2026-08-26) — Windrose Mod Settings slider fix
====================================================================
- Fixed: the Ghost Lifetime / Max Simultaneous Ghosts sliders in Settings > Mods now use the
  correct "slider" setting type (confirmed directly by Windrose Mod Settings' own author),
  with a proper step of 1 so the slider itself snaps to whole numbers. The previous release's
  best-guess type rendered a working slider, but with no snapping — landing on a value like
  "8.21 ghosts" was possible (harmless — it was always rounded before being applied — just
  confusing to look at).

====================================================================
1.0.0 (2026-08-26) — Initial release
====================================================================
- Home summons one random-look ghost sailor (Player Crew, Buccaneers Musketeer/Sailor/
  Sergeant, or a Brethren of the Coast woman) with a translucent ghost material, following you
  at pace, yielding to combat, warping back if left behind. (Home sits right next to End, by
  design.)
- End summons one corrupted Senkamati ally (Warrior/Hunter/Caster), same ghost look, real
  native combat AI intact, friendly to the player — fights alongside you.
- Both kinds dissipate (a short mist effect) and despawn after Config.GHOST_LIFETIME_MS
  (120s default), independently per summon.
- A trailing ground-light effect follows every active ghost.
- Shared max-active cap (Config.GHOST_MAX_ACTIVE, 5 default) across both kinds, with an
  on-screen "Max Ghosts Summoned" message when hit.
- Fully non-persistent: nothing this mod spawns is ever written to a save file.
- Fully standalone: no dependency on Living Base Enhanced at runtime.
- Optional Windrose Mod Settings integration: both hotkeys, ghost lifetime (seconds), max
  simultaneous ghosts, and the ground-light toggle are all editable in Settings > Mods.
